Our Story

About IronNow

Simplifying garment care across your city — one order at a time.

What is IronNow?

IronNow is a hyper-local, on-demand ironing marketplace that connects customers with trusted, professional ironing service providers in their neighbourhood — making garment care effortless from pickup to doorstep delivery.

The Problem We Solve

Traditional ironing shops demand inconvenient trips, long waits, and zero order visibility. Thousands of skilled local professionals also lack a digital platform to grow. IronNow bridges this gap — creating a two-sided marketplace that benefits everyone.

✅ For Customers

  • •Convenience — Book from home, any time
  • •Transparency — Real-time order tracking
  • •Quality — Only verified professionals
  • •Affordable — Per-garment, no hidden fees

🌱 For Professionals

  • •Steady Income — Continuous order stream
  • •Digital Presence — Grow a rated client base
  • •Flexible Hours — Work at your own pace
  • •Cashless — Secure, on-time payouts
