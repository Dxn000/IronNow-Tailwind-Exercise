About Our Platform

IronNow is a hyper-local platform that digitizes traditional neighborhood ironing services. It connects customers, ironing professionals, and delivery partners through a single online marketplace.

Users can schedule pickups, track their orders in real time, make digital payments, and receive neatly ironed clothes at their doorstep. The platform creates employment opportunities for local ironing professionals while providing unmatched convenience to customers.
